## Limits

This is static potential set coverage, not occupancy, hit rate, or a trace-driven cache simulation. It assumes a core can access all valid weights in its COUT slice; spike sparsity, access order, replacement, prefetch, traffic, and contention are not modeled. L2 is evaluated per core, not as a union across cores. Coverage does not establish that lines fit simultaneously or that ways are balanced. Capacity-bound here is a reachability feasibility test, not a cache-fit claim. Only the six captured workloads and sampled ways 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64 (when within line capacity) are covered; the stable threshold makes no claim about untested ways or other shapes.
